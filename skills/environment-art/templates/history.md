# History of <place>

Lore. The person approves this before any damage is placed.

1. **What was it?** Each part and what it was for. This fixes the routes people walked and where
   metal was fixed into stone.
2. **How was it built?** Materials, courses, what holds what up. This fixes how each part can fail.
3. **What happened, in order?** Three to five events, each with a place and the traces it left.

| # | Event | Where | Traces it left |
|---|---|---|---|

4. **Who uses it now?** One fresh trace laid over the rest.

**Routes on the plan:** <gate to gate, gate to centre, along the wall; where they cross or narrow>

**Prevailing wind and sun:** <which side sand banks on, which faces are bleached, which are in shade>

An event with no place or no traces is not finished.
